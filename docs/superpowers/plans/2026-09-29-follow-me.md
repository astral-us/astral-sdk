# Follow-Me Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute tasks in order with TDD on `feat/follow-me`. Keep the tracker, coordinator, command router, live adapters, and SwiftUI projection independently testable. Commit each green task or cohesive pair.

**Goal:** Add one-tap typed commands and an offline, rear-camera follow mode that acquires one person, maintains a 1.5-metre stand-off without switching to a newly central person, reacquires conservatively, and stops safely.

**Architecture:** Add a pure continuity tracker and a dedicated `FollowMeCoordinator` behind narrow perception, motion, and clock protocols. Feed it frame-coherent `RoverYOLO` detections and AR depth through a live adapter. Route finalized text and speech through a local `OperatorCommandRouter`, while retaining `MissionAgent` for ordinary commands. Project the coordinator state through a focused Talk view model into the existing `ConversationView`.

**Tech stack:** Swift 6, Observation, SwiftUI, XCTest/XCUITest, ARKit scene depth, Vision/Core ML `RoverYOLO`, PhroverKit, `NavigationController`, iOS 26 simulator, and a LiDAR-capable physical iPhone rover.

**Approved design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-follow-me-design.md`

## Global constraints

- Keep follow recognition, tracking, and control fully on-device.
- Use the rear-camera AR session; do not introduce front-camera capture or camera switching.
- Treat `Detector`/`RoverYOLO` as a person detector, not identity recognition.
- Select image-centre proximity only during initial acquisition. Never switch tracks merely because another person becomes central.
- Preserve the approved defaults: confidence 0.50, observation age 500 ms, 30-degree scan increments, 360-degree initial-search limit, 1.5-metre stand-off, 1.25–1.75-metre hold band, 0.30-metre goal-change threshold, at most three goal updates per second, two-second pose/depth recovery, and ten-second reacquisition.
- Reuse `NavigationController` safety and cancellation; do not issue raw rover-control commands from follow code.
- Exactly one subsystem may own motion. Follow mode and an ordinary `MissionAgent` mission must not run concurrently.
- Keep the existing push-to-talk interaction and route only its finalized transcript.
- Do not stage or overwrite unrelated working-tree files: `.serena/project.yml`, `.opencode/`, or `AGENTS.md`.

## Completion criteria

- Typed and finalized speech commands share one tested router, and typed commands work without speech permission or cloud connectivity.
- Follow mode acquires through at most one full rear-camera rotation and maintains the approved stand-off through existing navigation safety.
- A newly central person cannot steal an established lock; ambiguity pauses and then stops rather than guessing.
- Loss, stale data, AR interruption, safety failure, explicit stop, backgrounding, and teardown all leave rover motion cancelled.
- Focused SDK tests, app unit/UI tests, full SDK tests, device build, and physical acceptance all pass.
